# Woodgrain Bagels — cup mockups

Rotatable 3D mockups of the four cup SKUs, with design variants for each.

Open the page, pick a size across the top and a design in the right-hand column.
Drag the cup to rotate, scroll to zoom. **Fill** puts a light or dark drink in the
cup — worth trying on the clear cups, where whatever is behind the ink is its
background. **Lid** takes the lid off the hot cups.

### On colour

PMS Orange 021 C sits outside both CMYK and sRGB gamut. These renders use
`#FE5000`, the closest sRGB can reach — the real ink is more luminous than any
screen can show. **Approve the colour from a physical Pantone chip, not from
this page.**

### On dimensions

Cup dimensions are industry-standard assumptions, not manufacturer-confirmed.
Every design is re-projected onto whatever cone it is given, so real specs can be
dropped in and the artwork re-flows.

---

Built with three.js. Artwork is drawn flat and re-laid onto each cone so rows and
baselines read level around the cup — a cup sidewall unrolls to a curved wedge,
not a rectangle.
